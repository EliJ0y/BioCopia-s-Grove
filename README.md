# BioCopia-s-Grove
Welcome to BioCopia's Grove! After putting in your hard work and time into the levels of BioCopia, you have experience and money to buy things to decorate your Grove with. You may even encounter the animals of this Grove, provided you take good care of this place and leave out some treats for them!

This is a proof of concept for a section of an educational game (BioCopia) that is called the Grove to motivate people to complete levels. After people revise through the levels in BioCopia, this Grove will be a place for them to decorate and use rewards earned. This is made in a separate project to polish it before adding it to the main game itself. Although BioCopia is a game I am working on with my team, all programming for BioCopia's Grove is done by me.
